# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while communicating clearly.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Insults, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.

To report a concern, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainers through GitHub.
